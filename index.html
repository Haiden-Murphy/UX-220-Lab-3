Best Recent Movie

The best movie I saw recently was Resident Evil. Typically me and my best friend will go and see all the nerdy type movies and horror movies that look good. It just happens that the Resident Evil movie is based on a video game franchise, and it's a horror. We couldn't miss it.

Every once in a while I'll ask if my sister wants to tag along. with my friend and me. She decided to come with us, and she enjoyed the movie as well. Though there were mostly horror elements, there was also comedy, and it balanced it well. Jump scares were phenomenal, and tension building was insane.

One of my all-time favourite movies is Army of Darkness with Bruce Campbell; I love the raunchy horror-comedy mix. Though this movie wasn't really raunchy; it was grounded.
